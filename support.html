Common problems

Android says the setting is restricted

This happens with apps installed from outside the Play Store. Go to Settings, Apps, Doomky, tap the ⋮ at the top right, then Allow restricted settings. Go back and turn Doomky on.

Doomky keeps switching off

Some phones shut apps down to save battery. In Doomky, open You, the gear, then Keep Doomky running, and follow the steps for your phone.

The count looks wrong

Doomky counts each time you move to a new reel or Short inside Instagram or YouTube. If those apps change their screens, counting can break until we update Doomky. Please tell us which app and what you saw.

How do updates work?

Doomky tells you when a new version is ready. Download it from the home page again and install it over the old one. Your counts stay.

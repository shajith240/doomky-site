Delete your account

Last updated: 9 November 2026

How to permanently delete your Doomky account and the data linked to it, what is deleted, what is kept, and how long it takes.

This page is for the Doomky app for Android, made by Shajith Bathina. You can delete your account at any time, for free, and you do not need to give a reason.

1. Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Doomky on the phone where you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

You need to be online. The app only signs you out once our server has confirmed the account is deleted, so if it doesn't go through, you stay signed in and can try again.

2. Delete by email

If you can't open the app, for example because you uninstalled it or lost your phone, email us and we will delete the account for you.

Email: shajith240@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my Doomky account

  • Send it from the email address you sign in to Doomky with (for Google sign-in, your Google account's email).
  • Include your Doomky handle if you remember it.
  • If you write from a different address, we will ask you to confirm from the account's address first, so nobody can delete your account but you.

We will confirm by email once it is done.

3. What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes from our servers:

  • your account and email address;
  • your handle and friend code;
  • all your daily totals (reels, time, limit and whether Doomky was on);
  • your bananas and streak milestones;
  • your friends, friend requests you sent or received, and your place in every war;
  • wars you started, for everyone in them;
  • your list of blocked people, and reports other people made about you;
  • usage events that were linked to your account;
  • your push notification token.

You disappear from the leaderboard, from your friends' lists and from wars. This can't be undone. If you sign in again later, you start a new, empty account.

4. What is kept

  • Reports you made about other people. We keep the report to deal with abuse, but remove your account from it, so it no longer says who sent it.
  • Crash reports and anonymous usage events tied only to a random install ID. They never contained your name or email, and once your account is gone they can't be linked to you. Crash reports at Sentry are deleted automatically within 90 days.
  • Your phone's daily counts, without you. The link between your account and your phone is deleted, but the phone's daily reel counts (stored under a scrambled phone ID, never your name or email) stay. That stops anyone resetting a day by deleting and recreating an account.
  • Encrypted backups. Your data may remain in our encrypted nightly backups until they expire. Each backup is deleted after 14 days.
  • Anything we must keep by law, if a law or a valid legal order requires it. We are not currently aware of any such case.

5. How long it takes

WhatWhen
Deleting in the appImmediately, from our live database
Deleting by emailWithin 7 days of your request, or of you confirming it if we had to ask
Encrypted backupsWithin 14 days after deletion
Crash reports at SentryWithin 90 days (they contain no personal data)

6. If you never signed in

Then we hold no account for you. Your counts and settings are only on your phone. To delete them, uninstall Doomky, or go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Doomky > Storage > Clear storage.

Uninstalling also deletes the data on your phone if you did sign in, but it does not delete your account on our server. Delete the account first, using the steps above.

7. Other options

  • Sign out to keep your account but stop your phone sending anything to it.
  • Remove or block a friend to stop them seeing your counts.
  • Turn off the Accessibility service in your phone's settings to stop counting.
  • To get a copy of your data, or delete only part of it, email shajith240@gmail.com.

More detail is in our Privacy policy.

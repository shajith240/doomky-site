Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. This covers the Doomky app for Android.

The short version

Doomky works entirely on your phone unless you log in. It never reads your messages, captions or what you type. It has no ads and no analytics, and it never sells your data.

What the app sees

Doomky uses Android's Accessibility service, only inside Instagram and YouTube. From those apps it reads screen layout information: which screen is open and which reel or Short is in the middle of the screen. That's how it counts reels and shows your count. It does not read text, captions, comments, messages, or anything you type. It also uses this access to show the count on the edge of your screen and to show the stop screen at your limit.

What stays on your phone

  • Your daily reel counts and time spent on reels, split by app.
  • Your settings: daily limit, goal, language and promise.
  • If you use "Pay a friend": your friend's name, their UPI ID and the amount. Doomky only opens your own UPI app. It never handles or sees payments.

Uninstalling Doomky deletes all of this.

If you log in

Logging in is optional. You log in with Google, and Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Doomky. We store:

  • Your email address, to recognise your account.
  • The name you pick (your @handle).
  • Your daily totals: number of reels, time on reels, whether Doomky was switched on, and when each total was last updated.
  • The Friendly Wars you start or join.

We don't store your profile picture or your Google name.

Who can see it

  • The public leaderboard: anyone logged in to Doomky can see your @handle and your reel count for today and this week.
  • Friendly Wars: people in a war with you see your @handle, your totals during the war, and whether you're still reporting.
  • Nobody else sees your email.

Where it's kept

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and is sent over encrypted connections. The database only lets each person read and change their own data, apart from the leaderboard and war views above.

Other services

  • Google handles login, under Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub: the app checks GitHub for a newer version every few hours. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website visit.

Deleting your data

In Doomky, open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, name, totals and war memberships from our servers straight away. Logging out keeps your account, but stops sending anything from your phone.

Children

Doomky isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue.

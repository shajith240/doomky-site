Community guidelines

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The leaderboard is a race to watch less. Compete hard, keep it fair, and keep it friendly.

On this page
  1. Handles and war names
  2. Play fair
  3. Be kind
  4. Stay safe

  7. Appeals

These guidelines apply to everything other people can see on Doomky: your handle, the leaderboard, friends, friend requests and wars. They are part of our Terms of service.

1. Handles and war names

Your handle is shown to everyone on the leaderboard. Pick one you'd be happy for your teacher, your boss or your mum to see.

Fine

  • Nicknames, jokes, made-up words
  • Your first name or initials
  • Something about reels, monkeys or bananas

Not allowed

  • Slurs, hate, sexual or violent words
  • Pretending to be someone else, a brand, a creator or Doomky
  • Your phone number, address or anyone's personal details
  • Insults aimed at a real person

The same rules apply to the names you give wars. Some words are filtered automatically.

2. Play fair

Fewest reels wins, but only if everyone's count is real.

  • Don't modify the app or send fake counts to our servers.
  • Don't use extra accounts to push yourself up, or to boost or bury someone else.
  • Don't exploit bugs to earn bananas. If you find one, please tell us.

Switching Doomky off to dodge counting doesn't make you a cheat, but your friends will see that you went quiet.

3. Be kind

  • Roasting is the monkey's job. Don't harass, bully, threaten or shame other people.
  • Don't spam friend requests or war invites. If someone doesn't accept, leave it.
  • Don't use the report button to get back at someone. Report real problems only.

4. Stay safe

  • Only add friends you actually know. A friend sees your handle, your daily counts and when you go over your limit.
  • Pay a friend sends real money through your own UPI app. Only set it up with someone you know and trust, and only for amounts you both agreed. Doomky never asks you for money, your PIN or bank details. Anyone who does, using the Doomky name, is not us.
  • Never share your sign-in email link or one-time codes with anyone.

5. Block and report

On the leaderboard or a friend's entry, open the person's menu:

  • Block stops you seeing each other on the leaderboard and in search, and removes them as a friend.
  • Report sends their handle to us for review. They are not told who reported them.

For anything serious, such as threats or someone at risk, email shajith240@gmail.com as well. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (112 in India).

6. What we do

We review reports by hand. Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before, we may:

  • change or remove a handle or war name;
  • reset counts or bananas that were faked;
  • remove someone from the leaderboard or wars for a while;
  • close the account, for serious or repeated breaks of these rules.

Where the law requires, we will pass information to the authorities.

7. Appeals

If we acted on your account and you think we got it wrong, email shajith240@gmail.com with your handle and what happened. A person will look at it again and reply within 14 days.
